NOTE ON SOURCE SELECTION:
The official UNHCR Lebanon X account was not used because the Flash Updates provide the
primary structured public crisis updates during the study window and reduce duplication with
social media summaries. Flash Updates are the authoritative weekly output of UNHCR Lebanon's
response and contain embedded citizen-relevant guidance (WhatsApp channel, HELP website,
return risk advisories, shelter locations).

NOTE ON COVERAGE:
Five Flash Updates overlap the study window: FU#5 (Mar 30–Apr 5), FU#6 (Apr 6–12),
FU#7 (Apr 13–19), FU#8 (Apr 20–26), FU#9 (Apr 27–May 3).
Flash Update #6 (April 6–12, 2026) was NOT available in the source materials provided
and is excluded from this dataset. Four Flash Updates are included: FU#5, FU#7, FU#8, FU#9.

FLASH UPDATE #5
Period: 30 March – 5 April 2026
Document ID: UNHCR_001
========================================================

KEY FIGURES (as of 5 April 2026):
- Internally displaced persons: 137,774 in shelters
- Active shelters: 677
- Injuries since Oct 2023: ~8,000+ (cumulative)
- Fatalities since Oct 2023: ~2,500+ (cumulative)

KEY DEVELOPMENTS:
- Jnah airstrike (April 1): Israeli airstrike damaged 3 active collective shelters in Jnah area
  (Beirut), requiring emergency relocation of displaced families
- Hard-to-reach areas: Spotlight on families trapped in conflict-affected areas unable to
  access humanitarian assistance due to ongoing hostilities and access constraints
- Saida eviction risks: Displaced families in Sidon (Saida) facing eviction threats from
  temporary accommodation; UNHCR engaging with landlords and authorities
- Tyre airstrike: Israeli strike killed a father and his two sons in Tyre area

CASH AND LIVELIHOODS:
- Emergency cash assistance at USD 145 per household per month for registered displaced families
- UNHCR coordinating with WFP and partners on cash delivery to hard-to-reach households

COMMUNICATION CHANNELS (citizen-facing):
- SMS sent to 157,000 registered displaced families with updates and guidance
- WhatsApp information channel: 73,000 subscribers; guidance on shelter access, protection
  risks, and available assistance
- HELP website (help.unhcr.org/lebanon): self-service registration and information portal

PROTECTION:
- Monitoring of eviction risks in urban areas; legal assistance provided to at-risk families
- Gender-based violence risks elevated in collective shelter settings; response teams deployed

SHELTER OPERATIONS:
- UNHCR and partners supporting 677 collective shelters across Lebanon
- Emergency shelter NFI distributions ongoing for newly displaced

FLASH UPDATE #7
Period: 13–19 April 2026
Document ID: UNHCR_002
========================================================

KEY FIGURES (as of 19 April 2026):
- Internally displaced persons: 111,577 in shelters
- Active shelters: 659 (decline from prior week due to returns and consolidations)

KEY DEVELOPMENTS:
- High Commissioner visit: UNHCR High Commissioner Barham Salih visited Lebanon April 13–15;
  met with Lebanese authorities, displaced communities, and humanitarian partners; emphasized
  UNHCR commitment to protection during ongoing hostilities
- Ceasefire-period movements: Continued fluid population movements — some families attempting
  return to south Lebanon amid ongoing Israeli military presence and UXO contamination risks;
  UNHCR issuing guidance on return risks
- Return risk advisory: UNHCR strongly advising against premature return to areas with active
  UXO contamination, damaged infrastructure, and unresolved security conditions; directing
  families to verify safety through official channels before return
- Israeli military activity: Ongoing airstrikes and military operations in south Lebanon;
  displacement continuing from border areas

CHILD PROTECTION:
- 13 separated and unaccompanied children identified during reporting week
- 10 children successfully reunited with families through tracing mechanisms
- 3 children placed in interim care pending family tracing

SHELTER:
- Shelter numbers declining as some families return; consolidation of operational sites
- UNHCR monitoring spontaneous returns with field teams; safety assessments ongoing

COMMUNICATION CHANNELS:
- WhatsApp information channel: active; guidance on return risks and protection services
- HELP website: registration and shelter location information available

FLASH UPDATE #8
Period: 20–26 April 2026
Document ID: UNHCR_003
========================================================

KEY FIGURES (as of 26 April 2026):
- Internally displaced persons: 115,432 in shelters
- Active shelters: 631

KEY DEVELOPMENTS:
- EPCA cash assistance spotlight: Emergency Preparedness and Crisis Anticipation (EPCA)
  cash program expanded; direct transfers to households; Uhud story featured — individual
  displaced family beneficiary whose story illustrates impact of cash assistance on daily
  survival and dignity
- WhatsApp channel: 74,000 subscribers; 4 targeted messages sent during reporting week:
    1. ERW/UXO safety guidance (do not touch unexploded ordnance; report locations)
    2. Return risk advisory (south Lebanon conditions not yet safe for mass return)
    3. Child safety guidance (keeping children away from conflict-affected structures)
    4. Available assistance channels (HELP website + hotline referral)
- Shelter numbers: slight uptick (from 111,577 to 115,432) — new displacement from
  continued strikes offsetting returns

CASH AND LIVELIHOODS:
- EPCA cash: USD 145/household/month baseline; top-ups for most vulnerable
- Payments reaching households via e-transfer and partner cash points

PROTECTION:
- Continued monitoring of spontaneous returns; field teams in Tyre, Nabatieh, and Bekaa
- ERW contamination documented in returned areas; UNHCR liaising with LMAC on demining

COMMUNICATION CHANNELS (citizen-facing):
- WhatsApp: 74,000 subscribers; primary mass communication channel for UNHCR Lebanon
- HELP website (help.unhcr.org/lebanon): registration, shelter search, referral pathways
- Hotline referral included in weekly messaging

FLASH UPDATE #9
Period: 27 April – 3 May 2026
Document ID: UNHCR_004
========================================================

KEY FIGURES (as of 3 May 2026):
- Total displaced (IDP): 1,049,328
- Displaced in collective shelters: 119,623
- Active shelters: 626
- Total injuries (since Oct 2023): 8,020
- Total fatalities (since Oct 2023): 2,586
- Syrian nationals who crossed to Syria: 306,888
- Lebanese nationals who crossed to Syria: 64,865

KEY DEVELOPMENTS:
- Shelter numbers continuing to fluctuate; 119,623 in collective sites, others in informal
  arrangements (rented accommodations, staying with host families)
- Ongoing Israeli military activity documented; casualties and displacement continuing
- Cross-border movement: significant numbers of both Syrian and Lebanese nationals crossing
  into Syria — 306,888 Syrians and 64,865 Lebanese; UNHCR monitoring conditions at crossings
- Protection monitoring at border crossing points; documentation and referral services

SHELTER AND NFI:
- 626 collective shelters operational
- NFI distributions and hygiene kit distributions continuing
- Shelter rehabilitation and upgrade projects ongoing in priority sites

CASH ASSISTANCE:
- EPCA cash transfers continuing; coordinating with WFP on complementary food assistance
- Targeting criteria refined to reach most vulnerable displaced households

COMMUNICATION AND INFORMATION:
- WhatsApp channel: subscribers maintained at ~74,000+; weekly messaging continuing
- HELP website: updated with shelter information and registration pathways
- UNHCR field teams conducting outreach in shelter sites and return areas

CROSS-BORDER:
- 306,888 Syrian nationals returned to Syria from Lebanon during crisis period
- 64,865 Lebanese nationals crossed to Syria (temporary refuge or onward movement)
- UNHCR monitoring conditions; voluntary repatriation programs suspended pending
  security conditions in Syria
